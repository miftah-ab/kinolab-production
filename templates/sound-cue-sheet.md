## Mix priorities

1. Dialogue / voice intelligibility (when present).
2. Essential product and action sounds.
3. Music emotional support.
4. Background ambience.

## Final listening review

- [ ] No clipping or distortion.
- [ ] Voice is intelligible.
- [ ] Music does not mask important information.
- [ ] Foley is synchronized and plausible.
- [ ] SFX are purposeful and restrained.
- [ ] No clicks, gaps or abrupt unwanted endings.
- [ ] Full export listened to on headphones and phone speaker.
- [ ] Commercial-use rights checked.
