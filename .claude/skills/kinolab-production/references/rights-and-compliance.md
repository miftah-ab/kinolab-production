# Rights, Claims, Consent and AI Disclosure

This is a production risk checklist, not legal advice. Laws and platform policies vary by country, product category, placement and date. Check current official requirements before publishing.

## Client and asset permissions

Track permission for:
- Client name, logo, trademark, product and packaging.
- Filming location, private property and identifiable interiors.
- Employees, actors, customers, children and other identifiable people.
- Voice, likeness, performance and testimonials.
- Stock footage, music, SFX, fonts, templates and third-party design assets.
- AI-generated content and the selected provider's commercial terms.

Record source, creator/provider, licence/permission, allowed media/territory/duration, attribution and proof location. Do not assume "free", "AI-generated", "royalty-free" or "available online" means unrestricted commercial use.

## Claims and endorsements

- Use only substantiated claims supplied or approved by the client.
- Keep proof for comparative, health, environmental, financial, safety, performance and superiority claims.
- Never invent testimonials, ratings, customer counts, awards, scarcity, endorsements or before/after results.
- Disclose material connections and endorsements where applicable.
- Avoid implying that a real person used, approved or endorsed a product when they did not.
- Escalate regulated or high-risk categories for qualified review.

## AI-generated and synthetic media

Check the current platform disclosure rules. YouTube currently requires disclosure for realistic altered/generated content that could mislead viewers about real people, events or places, and certain other categories. Do not assume every AI-assisted edit requires the same disclosure or that disclosure permits otherwise prohibited content.

Do not create deceptive depictions of real people, fake news footage, false testimonials or misleading evidence. Keep a record of what was generated/altered and what is authentic.

## Final approval

Before delivery, confirm who approved claims, likeness/voice use, music/asset rights, platform disclosures and final creative. Flag unresolved permissions clearly; do not silently mark the project cleared.
