# Start New Production — Client Intake

## Conversation rules
Ask one question per turn in plain language. Never dump the questionnaire. Skip answers already supplied in chat or visible assets. Let the user say "you decide" for creative choices; record the chosen default. Ask only questions that change scope, message, production method, cost or delivery. Never invent critical facts. Distinguish client commission from spec/portfolio exercise.

## Intake sequence
1. What are we making: business ad, product ad, restaurant/café promo, hotel/property film, brand film, event promo or other?
2. What format: vertical 9:16, horizontal 16:9, square 1:1 or multiple? Where will it be published?
3. Which business/product/service? Name, category, offer, location if relevant, and exact product/experience.
4. What is the goal: awareness, launch, offer, visits, messages, bookings, orders or another action?
5. Who is the audience: customer type, market/geography, need/desire and language?
6. What is the key selling point? Ask for proof/source if it is a factual claim.
7. What assets exist: product photos, logo, brand guide, location footage, existing video, staff/actors, testimonials or none? Ask for uploads when useful.
8. Desired style: premium/cinematic, energetic, minimal/elegant, emotional/story-driven, documentary or "you decide".
9. Duration: recommend based on platform and message; 15, 30 or 60 seconds are options, not rules.
10. Voice/sound: voiceover, dialogue, music-led, natural sound or combination. Ask language/accent only if voice is needed.
11. Production method/constraints: real footage, AI visuals, Blender 3D or hybrid; available tools; deadline; budget including zero; paid tools/assets allowed?
12. Project status/authority: client work or spec? Who approves claims, logo, location, likeness/voice, final cut?

## Minimum viable brief
Project type/status; business/product and verified facts; audience; objective/message/CTA; destination/ratio; duration or permission to recommend; assets and production constraints; rights/permission status.

## Confirm before concept
Summarize project/status, business truth, audience, objective/message/CTA, destination/ratios/duration, creative direction, assets, production method/tools/budget, sound/voice, deadline/approver, assumptions/open questions and rights risks. Ask for approval/corrections. Creative discretion does not authorize spending or unlicensed use.

## Avoid
No lens/frame-rate/font questions at intake. Do not force logo, voiceover, 3D or AI video. An uploaded photo is not proof of a claim. Do not publish real business assets or identifiable people in spec work without permission or a defensible basis. Never fabricate discounts, scarcity, rankings, reviews, ingredients, certifications or results.
