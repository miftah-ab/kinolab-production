# Start New Production — Client Intake

## Conversation rules

- Ask one question per turn, in plain language.
- Never show all questions as a form or long list unless the user asks for a form.
- Skip answers already supplied in chat or visible assets.
- Let the user say "you decide" for creative choices; choose a sensible default and record it.
- Ask only questions that affect scope, message, production method, cost or delivery.
- If a critical fact is unknown, ask; do not invent it.
- Distinguish a client commission from a spec/portfolio exercise.

## Intake sequence

1. **What are we making?** A business advertisement, product ad, restaurant/café promo, hotel/property film, brand film, event promo, or something else?
2. **What format?** Vertical 9:16, horizontal 16:9, square 1:1, or multiple formats? Ask where it will be published if unclear.
3. **What business/product/service?** Name, category, what it offers, location if relevant, and the exact product/experience to feature.
4. **What is the goal?** Awareness, product launch, offer promotion, visits, calls/messages, bookings, orders or another action?
5. **Who is the audience?** Customer type, market/geography, need/desire and any language requirements.
6. **What is the key selling point?** What makes this offer worth attention? Ask for proof or source if it is a factual claim.
7. **What assets are available?** Product photos, logo, brand guide, location footage, existing video, staff/actors, testimonials, or none. Ask user to attach files where useful.
8. **What style should it have?** Premium/cinematic, energetic, minimal/elegant, emotional/story-driven, documentary, or "you decide".
9. **How long?** Recommend a focused duration based on platform and message; common starting points are 15, 30 or 60 seconds, not mandatory rules.
10. **Voice and sound?** Voiceover, dialogue, music-led, natural sound, or a combination. Ask language/accent only if voice is needed.
11. **Production method and constraints?** Real footage, AI visuals, Blender 3D, mixed workflow; tools available; deadline; budget including zero; and whether any paid tools/assets are prohibited.
12. **Project status and approvals?** Is this paid client work or a spec/portfolio concept? Who can approve product claims, logo use, location filming, people/voice likeness and final delivery?

## Minimum viable brief

Do not force all twelve answers for a simple project. Minimum essentials:
- Project type and client/spec status.
- Business/product/service and verified facts.
- Audience and market.
- Objective, key message and desired CTA.
- Platform/destination and aspect ratio.
- Duration or permission for Claude to recommend.
- Assets and production constraints.
- Rights/permission status for important third-party assets.

## Brief confirmation

Summarize:
- Project name and status.
- Business/product and verified facts.
- Audience/market.
- Objective, key message and CTA.
- Destination, aspect ratio(s), duration.
- Creative direction.
- Assets available and missing.
- Production method, tools and budget.
- Voice/music/sound preference.
- Deadline and approval contact/authority.
- Assumptions, open questions and rights risks.

Ask the user to approve or correct the brief before creative development. If they explicitly authorize Claude to decide creative details, record that but do not treat it as permission to spend money or use unlicensed assets.

## Intake pitfalls

- Do not ask for lens choice, frame rate or font before knowing the project.
- Do not force a logo, voiceover, 3D or AI video into every ad.
- Do not treat an uploaded photo as proof of a claim.
- Do not publish real business logos, locations, customers or staff in spec work without permission or a defensible basis.
- Never fabricate discounts, scarcity, rankings, awards, reviews, product benefits, ingredients, certifications or customer results.
