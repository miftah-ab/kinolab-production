# Commercial Strategy and Creative Development

## Audience and buyer
Define the target customer, market, buying context, need/desire, objection, awareness level and desired next action. Separate what the client says from what evidence supports.

## Ad angle
Build a single clear angle from a real customer insight and verified product truth. A useful short ad often contains a hook, relevance/desire, proof or demonstration, payoff and one CTA. These are functions, not a rigid five-scene formula.

## Concept options
When it helps, show no more than three genuinely different concepts. Each includes title, audience insight, message, hook, visual device, CTA, production complexity and likely cost drivers. Recommend one and explain the trade-off.

## Short-form creative
Make the first meaningful visual or message arrive immediately. Show the product or service early enough that the viewer understands the subject. Remove pretty shots that add no new information, emotion, proof or progression. Keep one primary message and CTA.

## Truth and conversion
Never promise sales, views, bookings or ROI. Do not invent features, awards, rankings, testimonials, customer counts, discounts, scarcity or comparative superiority. Use client-approved evidence. Match CTA to a real channel: visit, call, message, book, order or learn more. Never fake proof or endorsements.
