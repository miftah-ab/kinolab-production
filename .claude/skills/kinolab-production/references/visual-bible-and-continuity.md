# Visual Bible and Continuity

Create a visual bible for every sequence with recurring subjects, products, locations, characters or distinctive art direction.

## Lock

- Product truth: proportions, dimensions, materials, labels, colors, packaging and logo placement.
- Character/talent: approved appearance, wardrobe, hair, age range, features and permitted variation.
- Environment: architecture, set dressing, surfaces, props, weather and time of day.
- Art direction: palette, contrast, texture, mood and reference principles.
- Camera: framing family, height, perspective, depth of field and movement rules.
- Lighting: source direction, softness, contrast and practical lights.
- Post look: color transform, grade, grain and finishing.
- Sound palette: music character, ambience, Foley and signature sounds.

## Reference roles

Label each reference as product identity, character identity, location/set, composition, lighting/mood, style, or prop reference. State precedence when references conflict. Product identity and verified brand facts outrank aesthetic inspiration.

## Continuity controls

- Reuse approved references where the selected model supports them.
- Repeat essential identity details consistently across relevant prompts.
- Track wardrobe, props, orientation, labels, hand positions, liquid/food state, screen direction and lighting.
- Keep generated text/logos/UI out of shots where accuracy matters; add them in post.
- Treat continuity as a review requirement, not a promise from the model.
