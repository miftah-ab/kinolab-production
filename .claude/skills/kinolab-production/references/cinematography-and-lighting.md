# Cinematography, Camera and Lighting

## Camera language

Choose shot size and angle to communicate purpose:
- Wide/establishing: place, environment and context.
- Medium: human interaction and product use.
- Close-up: expression, material, detail and decision.
- Macro/detail: texture, craftsmanship, food, liquid, surface and mechanism.
- Overhead/top-down: layout, ingredients, tabletop and spatial organization.
- Low/high angle: use deliberately to influence scale or status, not as random variety.

For each shot, define subject size, camera height, angle, perspective, focus plane, depth of field and camera movement. Lens values are descriptive guidance, not guaranteed model controls.

## Movement

One primary camera move per generated shot. Specify:
- Start position and end position.
- Direction and speed.
- Whether subject or camera moves.
- What must remain stable.
- Whether focus changes.
Avoid simultaneous orbit + zoom + crane + handheld unless a real operator can execute it and the effect is intentional.

## Lighting

Describe source, direction, size/softness, contrast, color temperature relationship, practical sources, reflections and background separation. For products, control specular highlights and preserve label readability. For food, preserve believable color and texture; do not make it look plastic. For interiors, preserve window/exterior exposure and vertical lines.

## Live-action notes

For real shoots, specify available light, fixtures, diffusion/negative fill, bounce, practicals, exposure consistency, white balance, focus, stabilization and clean room tone. Keep camera settings achievable with the user's equipment; ask what camera/phone is available before exact settings.

## Continuity and color

Keep screen direction, eyelines, lighting direction, white balance, exposure, focal length family and camera height coherent across cuts. Do not force a teal-orange grade or film emulation onto every brand. Protect natural skin tones and product color.

## Reference research

Use image references for the exact purpose stated (composition, lighting, lensing, materials, location, product identity). Do not copy another brand's protected campaign wholesale. Describe the visual principles and create an original treatment.
