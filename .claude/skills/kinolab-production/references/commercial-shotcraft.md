# Commercial Shotcraft by Subject

Adapt technique to the thing being sold. Do not use one generic AI-video recipe for every business.

## Physical products

- Establish exact product identity from approved references: dimensions/proportions, material, finish, label, packaging, cap/closure, logo placement and color.
- Use controlled hero, detail, interaction, scale and in-context shots.
- Avoid invented features, impossible mechanisms, incorrect labels or changed packaging.
- Keep text/logo overlays for post-production where precision matters.
- For liquids, glass and reflective products, specify reflection control, refraction, condensation and liquid state.

## Food and beverages

- Prioritize appetite appeal through credible texture, steam, condensation, pour, cut, stretch, sizzle and plating.
- Keep ingredients, portions, colors and presentation truthful to the actual menu item.
- Track state continuity: whole/cut, filled/empty, hot/cold, garnish, utensil and hand positions.
- Avoid uncanny food texture, fake steam, implausible liquid motion and over-saturated color.
- Capture clean Foley where possible: pour, crisp bite, sizzle, plate, cutlery, package opening.
- Do not invent health, nutrition, origin or ingredient claims.

## Restaurants, cafés and hospitality

- Sell a real experience: arrival, atmosphere, service, signature product, human warmth and useful location/booking CTA.
- Get permission for identifiable staff/customers and private interiors.
- Show the real venue and menu accurately. Do not fabricate views, facilities or services.

## Hotels and property

- Preserve real layout, dimensions, room count, amenities, view, fixtures and access.
- Avoid AI-generated architecture that misrepresents the property.
- Use coherent spatial progression and plausible camera movement.
- Verify every feature and location claim; label conceptual CGI when it is not a documentary depiction.

## People and talent

- Confirm consent and usage scope for likeness, voice and performance.
- Do not clone or imitate a person's voice without appropriate authorization.
- Keep wardrobe, appearance, props, eyelines and gestures consistent.
- Do not create fake testimonials, endorsements or implied customer experiences.

## Brand films

- Define a central brand idea and a visual system that can extend across multiple deliverables.
- Keep brand values grounded in client-approved language and proof.
- Avoid vague montage where no specific audience message or action emerges.
