## Mix priorities
1. Dialogue/voice intelligibility when present.
2. Essential product/action sounds.
3. Music emotional support.
4. Background ambience.

## Review
- [ ] Voice clear and correctly pronounced.
- [ ] Music supports the ad without masking detail.
- [ ] Foley synchronized and believable.
- [ ] SFX purposeful and restrained.
- [ ] No clipping, distortion, clicks or gaps.
- [ ] Full export heard on headphones and phone speaker.
- [ ] Loudness/true peak measured if required.
- [ ] Commercial-use rights checked.
