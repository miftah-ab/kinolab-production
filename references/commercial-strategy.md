# Commercial Strategy and Creative Development

## Start with the buyer and business

Before proposing visuals, understand:
- What is being sold?
- Who is most likely to buy or visit?
- What desire, problem, occasion or objection matters?
- What differentiates the offer, based on verified facts?
- What should the viewer do after watching?
- Where will the ad run and what is the viewing context?

## Build a clear ad angle

A useful short commercial usually has:
1. **Hook:** A visual or statement that earns attention immediately.
2. **Desire / problem / context:** Why the audience should care.
3. **Proof or demonstration:** Show the real product, service, space, experience or verified benefit.
4. **Payoff:** Make the value easy to understand.
5. **CTA:** One simple next action.

Not every ad needs a voiceover or all five parts as separate scenes. Preserve clarity and pacing.

## Concept options

When useful, present up to three genuinely different concepts, not three rewrites of the same idea. For each include:
- Working title.
- Audience insight.
- Core promise / message.
- Hook.
- Visual device.
- CTA.
- Production complexity and likely cost drivers.

Recommend one option and explain why. Do not make the user choose among many trivial variations.

## Commercial honesty

- Never guarantee sales, views, virality, bookings or ROI.
- Never invent product features, awards, testimonials, customer counts, prices, discounts, ingredients, certifications or availability.
- Use placeholders for unverified claims and request proof.
- A spec/portfolio ad must be clearly identified as a concept, not client work.
- If the business is not a real client, avoid implying endorsement or permission. Use fictionalized branding or obtain permission before publishing a real business's logo, location or product likeness.
- Make the CTA appropriate to the actual business channel (visit, call, message, book, order, learn more).

## Short-form pacing

For 15–30 second ads, avoid long introductions. Make the first meaningful visual or message arrive immediately. Each shot must add new information, emotion, proof, or visual progression. Remove shots that only look beautiful but do not serve the ad.
