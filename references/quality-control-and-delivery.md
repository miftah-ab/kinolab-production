# Quality Control, Rights and Delivery

## Human visual review

Inspect every shot for:
- Product fidelity: packaging, labels, logo, shape, color, scale and features.
- Identity and continuity: people, wardrobe, props, environment and lighting.
- Physical plausibility: hands, object interaction, gravity, reflections and motion.
- Generative defects: warping, flicker, morphing, extra objects, texture crawl, unstable text.
- Cinematography: composition, focus, exposure, camera movement and screen direction.
- Commercial clarity: product visible, message understandable, CTA legible.
- Edit quality: pacing, rhythm, transitions, crop and safe areas.
- Color: consistency and faithful product/skin/food appearance.

## Human audio review

Listen to the full export on headphones and a phone speaker where possible:
- Voice is intelligible and correctly pronounced.
- Music supports rather than overwhelms the message.
- Ambience and Foley sound plausible and synchronized.
- SFX are intentional, not excessive.
- No clipping, distortion, clicks, silence gaps or abrupt endings.
- Audio fades and ending resolve cleanly.

## Technical checks

Verify the exported file rather than trusting editor settings:
- File opens and plays from beginning to end.
- Correct aspect ratio, resolution, frame rate and duration.
- Correct codec/container for delivery destination.
- No black frames, frozen frames, missing shots or accidental blank sections.
- Audio stream exists when expected; sample rate/channels are acceptable.
- No unintended watermark.
- File name matches the agreed naming convention.
- Check file size and playback compatibility.

Use ffprobe/FFmpeg only when installed and available. If not available, provide a manual inspection path. Never claim automated checks passed unless they were run and their output reviewed.

## Rights and compliance

Before delivery/publishing, confirm rights or permission for:
- Client logo, product, location, people and supplied footage.
- Stock footage, fonts, music, sound effects, templates and generated assets under their applicable terms.
- Model-generated content restrictions and disclosure requirements for the intended platform.
- Claims, prices, discounts, testimonials and regulated categories.
- Real people, likenesses and trademarks.

Do not provide legal certainty. Flag material licensing or advertising risks and recommend checking the actual license or getting qualified advice when needed.

## Delivery checklist

Deliver only the formats approved in the brief. Include:
- Final export(s) with clear names.
- Optional project/source files if agreed.
- Asset manifest and licenses/attribution if applicable.
- A concise note listing formats, duration and any known limitations.
- Final QA status: Passed, Passed with noted limitations, or Not approved.

Never call a video "final" if it has not received human review.
